Hobbies

Snowboarding

I have been snowboarding since I was 8. I started out at Gunstock Mountatin in New Hampshire, my grandfather's favorite ski mountain. Recently, however, I have been going to Loon and Killington in Vermont. I am lucky enough to go on a skiing trip out West with my dad, grandfather, and brother every year. Destinations include Jackson Hole, Big Sky, Steamboat, Telluride, Aspen, and most recently Sun Valley. Every time we go, I make sure to get a sticker to add to my snowboard, turning it into a memory collage of sorts. My favorite kind of trails are the powdery glades or small terrain parks (I just hit a rail for the first time!).

Basketball

I am one of my team's social chairs. I am in charge of bondings, team spirit, and social media. My favorite thing about Tufts University Women's Club Basketball is that we all work incredibly hard while lifting eachother up. It is a fun space where we listen to music while we play, sometimes dancing or singing along. Though I have played basketball since I was 5, I have never experienced a team so welcoming and safe. Also, it's super fun to win!

Reading

Reading is one of my favorite things to do right before going to sleep, when I need to relax, or when I need a break from staring at screens. I enjoy all genres, including fiction, fantasy, mystery, and greek mythology. What really stands out to me in books are character development and the ability to make me feel like I'm in the book.

See of 4 of my favorite books: "What If?" by Randall Munroe "The Da Vinci Code" by Dan Brown, "1984" by George Orwell, and "The Song of Achilles" by Madeline Miller.

"Science Vs" (Podcast by Spotify Studios)

Wendy Zukerman, the host of "Science Vs," spends each episode talking about a different random "fact." She introduces the "fact" and then shares her research in finding out if that fact is actually covered by science, and how much. The catch-phrase of this show is "There are a lot of fads, blogs and strong opinions, but then there's SCIENCE."

I have always been a fan for fun facts, and each episode is an extremly in depth version of a fun fact. Topics I have learned about include AI, chriopractors, seed oils, daylight savings, and so many more. I find it to be a nice break from my typical STEM-filled day, and will listen while I'm walking to class, cooking, doing homework, or working out. I highly recommend!

"Rick and Morty" (TV show by Adult Swim)

I love this show not only becuase it's funny, but because it touches upon many philosophical ideas, such as nihilism and absurdism, in a palatable way.
