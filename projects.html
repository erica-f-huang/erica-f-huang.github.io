Mechanical Keyboard

SUMMARY: My first mechanical keyboard build! I did months of research before buying the parts because there are so many different kinds of layouts, swithces, keycaps, and other specifications. I had some trouble testing the PCB, but ended up getting everything working in the end. Super fun build and hopefully not my last.
SKILLS: using/ testing PCB, troubleshooting, knowledge about mechanical keyboards
TAKEAWAYS: No matter how much research you do, the only way to truly learn is by doing
PARTS:
  Barebones kit: Bakenko65
  Switches: HMX Butter Linears
  Keycaps: Keith Haring Inspired Keycap Set
